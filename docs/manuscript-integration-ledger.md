# Manuscript integration ledger

Tracks full-text manuscript reading editions (distinct from the bounded preview-adaptation
queue in [`release-candidate-registry.md`](./release-candidate-registry.md) and from the
author-paper synopsis catalogue). Columns: **source/version → review decision → website route →
GitHub release → verified live status**, kept as separate, honestly-distinct states per the
owner's explicit instruction not to collapse them into one.

**Scope note (corrected 2026-09-11):** an earlier revision of this ledger stated that this
session had no access to the local Dropbox volumes or `private/audit/*`. That was wrong — a
Dropbox connector is available to this session, and `/Independent-Observer-Clarity-20260909`
and `/Independent-Observer-Taxonomy-20260909` (including `private/audit/extractions-*.json`,
`manuscript-review-20260910.json`, `curated-families.json`, and `family-assignments.json`) were
read directly from it to prepare the two editions below. The remaining boundary is scope, not
access: with 140 research families audited and only the four discussed by name in the owner's
source-review notes (Manifesto of a Destiny, The Security of Memory, Quiet Wealth, Reputation
Debt) individually reviewed for full-text release, expanding the other ~136 families from
taxonomy summaries to full reading editions is a large remaining body of work this session did
not attempt in this pass — recorded as an open item below, not silently left out.

  release-authorization gate), verify with the full unit + e2e suite, push, reply to and resolve
  each review thread, merge, and verify the live deployment — twice, once for PR #48's merge
  commit and once for the follow-up ledger-correction commit.
- Did (2026-09-11): read the Dropbox workspace directly (a connector is available to this
  session — the earlier claim that it was not is corrected above), located the exact source
  extractions for Security of Memory (idx 24), Quiet Wealth (idx 36), and Reputation Debt
  (idx 31) in `private/audit/extractions-000.json`, cross-checked them against
  `manuscript-review-20260910.json`, `curated-families.json`, and `family-assignments.json`, and:
  - prepared and integrated reading editions of Quiet Wealth and Reputation Debt with exactly the
    edits the owner's source-review notes specified, each recorded with its own
    release-authorization entry, family ID, taxonomy link, and test coverage;
  - confirmed Security of Memory's existing "do not release full text yet" decision and left it
    at taxonomy-summary level, matching the recorded reason (author-ID placeholders and
    unverified claims) rather than publishing it or inventing a different disposition.
- Did not: attempt full-text review and integration for the other ~136 research families in the
  140-family taxonomy. That is a large body of work beyond the four papers the owner's
  source-review notes named individually, and is recorded above as an open scope item.
- Did not (yet, this pass): produce a dated revision of `MASTER_INTEGRATION_MAP.docx` /
  `START_HERE_INTEGRATION_MAP.md` reflecting the two new integrations — see the Dropbox workspace
  for whether a revision was completed after this ledger entry was written; this ledger is the
  in-repository, git-tracked record and complements but does not replace those two documents.
