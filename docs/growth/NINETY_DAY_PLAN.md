# Independent Observer — 90-day controlled growth cycle

**Status:** proposed schedule beginning after release authorization and reliable baseline collection. No publishing promise is made to readers.

| Window | Work | Verification gate | Owner decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–14 | Capture main/production SHA, record index/SEO access, open a private aggregate scorecard, and check three mobile first-read journeys | Sitemap/robots/live build checks; five real first-time-reader observations; documented gaps | Approve privacy-safe metrics source and pilot readers |
| Days 15–30 | Improve the 3 highest-friction published article entry paths and external newsletter explanations; preserve core texts | Citation/version comparisons and iPhone accessibility checks; confirmed Substack destination | Approve presentation changes and draft distributions |
| Days 31–45 | Prepare first AI-work evidence package using released sources; test Substack + one channel | Canonical links and accurate factual claims; source-backed format assets; actual publication status | Approve actual posting/publishing |
| Days 46–60 | Prepare accountability package only after adequate public-record sourcing; repair observed discovery problems | Document review, counterarguments and clear uncertainty; audit index/query changes | Author review of factual and legal claims |
| Days 61–75 | Evaluate acquisition, reading paths, and newsletter deltas | Comparable dates/definitions; NOT VERIFIED where unavailable | Reallocate effort based on actual results |
| Days 76–90 | Repeat tests, close defects, conduct Council comparison, decide next quarter's two themes | Completed checklists, traceable QA and evidence; no score inflation | Author approves site release and following quarter |

## Minimum deliverable per completed cycle

An approved canonical article or revision; its original evidence and correction history; one concise public explanation; one optional social or audio adaptation; a record of labor spent; and a dated measurement memo. A missing outcome record is unknown, not failure. A channel with zero verified tracking must not be reported as zero audience.

## Success criteria

Success means measurable lower reader friction, attributable improvement in credible discovery where source data permits, and demonstrable voluntary repeat readership. No subscriber target is promised until a validated baseline exists. Site-readiness targets above 8/10 are aspirational and require actual checks, not more features.
