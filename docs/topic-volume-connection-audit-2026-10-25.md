The map is deliberately evidence-led. “Connected” means the public catalogue has a relevant roadmap, article, author-paper synopsis, or clearly marked preview for that topic and volume. ## Publication and source boundaries

- The table distinguishes published articles, author working-paper synopses, concept previews, documentary previews, and roadmaps. An author-paper synopsis is not presented as the complete manuscript or as independent peer review.
- The reconciled Dropbox ledger assigns _The Welfare Paradox_ a Volume II candidate placement but also says its source-family mapping still needs confirmation. It is therefore not used to claim a Volume II or Volume III cell connection pending that reconciliation.
- The six unmapped cells remain explicit: History–IV, Law–IV, Science–I/II/III, and Technology–II. Additions require a suitable public entry point and a checked volume relationship.
- The Dropbox curation audit describes 10,594 current file records and 1,742 legacy-comparison records, with targeted text extraction rather than a line-by-line review of every manuscript. This implementation reconciles the public catalogue and the available audit ledger; it does not claim a fresh source audit of every private volume file. The next source-audit pass should compare the remaining volume families and any held adaptations with their controlling manuscripts before adding further cells or changing statuses.
